# Pre-sales page

Single-page landing page you send a prospect the day before a sales call.

## Edit for a new prospect

1. Open `config.js`. That is the only file you need to change for copy, logos, and the CTA.
2. Drop the new logo and headshot into `assets/`.
3. Click **Run**, then **Publish**, and send the live URL.

Do not add sections. Keep the seven blocks in this order: Hero, What we noticed, Gap, Agenda, Proof, Who you are working with, CTA.

## Restyle in Replit Design

Select **Design**, attach this page, and paste `design-prompt.txt` after filling the brackets. Keep the same block order.
